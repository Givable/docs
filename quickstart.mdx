## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  To get started with Givable, ensure you have:

  - A valid email address for account registration

  - Nonprofit organization details (EIN or equivalent for verification)

  - A payment processor account (e.g., Stripe) for receiving donations
</Callout>

## Create Your Account

Follow these steps to sign up and access your dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Visit [https://www.givable.com](https://www.givable.com) and click "Request a Demo" or "Sign Up".

    Fill in your organization's details, including name, email, and nonprofit status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Givable.

    Click the link to confirm your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Onboarding" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Log in at [https://dashboard.givable.com](https://dashboard.givable.com).

    Connect your payment processor and configure basic settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Dashboard Walkthrough

Once logged in, the Givable dashboard provides an intuitive interface to manage forms, campaigns, and donations.

<Image src="https://www.givable.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/gvbl_donation_form.jpg" width="1024" height="600" alt="Givable dashboard showing customizable donation forms" />

Key sections include:

- **Forms**: Create and edit donation forms

- **Campaigns**: Launch fundraising pages

- **Donors**: View recurring and one-time gifts

- **Integrations**: Connect to tools like Salesforce NPSP or webhooks

## Build Your First Donation Form

Create a basic form in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start New Form" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    From the dashboard, click "New Form".

    Choose a template or start blank.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize Fields" icon="edit-3" title-type="p">
    Add fields for donor name, amount, payment method (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay).

    Enable recurring options for monthly giving.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and Save" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    Preview on desktop and mobile.

    Save as draft or publish directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Image src="https://www.givable.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/gvbl_form_upgrade.jpg" width="1024" height="600" alt="Givable donation form with gift upgrade to recurring" />

## Publish and Share Your Form

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Direct Link" icon="link">
    After publishing, copy the form URL (e.g., `https://donate.givable.com/formid`).

    Share via email, social media, or your website.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Embed Code" icon="code">
    Generate embed code for seamless integration.

    <CodeGroup show-lines="true" tabs={[]}>
      ```html
      <iframe src="https://forms.givable.com/your-org/form-123" width="100%" height="600" frameborder="0"></iframe>
      ```

      ```javascript
      // Load form dynamically
      const formId = 'your-org/form-123';
      const container = document.getElementById('donation-form');
      container.innerHTML = `<iframe src="https://forms.givable.com/${formId}" width="100%" height="600"></iframe>`;
      ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Test your form with a small donation to ensure payments process correctly before promoting widely.
</Callout>
