## Overview

Givable lets you structure your docs using spaces and folders.

## Steps

1. From your Givable dashboard, click "New Space".
2. Name your documentation space (e.g., "API Docs").
3. Create folders inside a space for categories (guides, API, FAQs).
4. Drag and drop pages to reorder.

## Tip

Use clear naming for spaces and folders to keep navigation simple.
