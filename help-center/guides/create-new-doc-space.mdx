## Overview

Create a space to organize your docs for a project or team.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Dashboard">
    Click on your avatar and select "Dashboard" from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add New Space">
    Click "New Space" and enter a name (e.g., "Marketing Docs").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Pages and Folders">
    Create new pages or folders inside your space to start adding content.
  </Step>
</Steps>
